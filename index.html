About Me

I’m a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Biomedical Informatics & Data Science at Yale University, advised by Prof. Qingyu Chen, and supported by the Yale-Boehringer Ingelheim Biomedical Data Science Fellowship. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Lehigh University, where I was very fortunate to be advised by Prof. Lifang He, Prof. Yu Zhang, and Prof. Brian Y. Chen. My research focuses on Deep Learning (Generative Modeling, Contrastive Learning, Cross-Modal Attention), Multimodal Learning, Medical Image Computing, and Brain Imaging Genomics.

Research Interests

  • Medical AI: Medical multimodal learning, generative modeling, and interpretable neural networks for disease prediction and clinical decision support
  • Knowledge Graphs and LLMs: Knowledge graph completion, semantic relation extraction, and integration with large language models for intelligent reasoning in educational and biomedical domains
  • Multimodal Data Integration: Fusion and representation learning across imaging, text, and genomics, with applications in diagnosis, biomarker discovery, and personalized recommendation
